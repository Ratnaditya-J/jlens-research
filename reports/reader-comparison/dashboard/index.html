Milestones, end to end

Goal: compare detection, interpretation and blind spots on confirmed behavioral violations, with controls and traceable evidence.

What we know so far

Qwen · same subject, all three methods

No overall winner

During-action blind Oracle detects more violations but produces more false alarms. After-action contextual success is matched by context-only and shuffled controls.

Qwen · early probe detections

Six probe-only positives have a shared-prefix confound

They share three prefixes and identical scores with 16 benign continuations. This does not establish an episode-specific hidden-intent direction.

Original GPT-OSS · 71 benign controls

J-lens summaries: 0 / 71 false alarms · probe: 71 / 71

A specificity advantage on these authored controls. It does not measure sensitivity or establish broad superiority.

Original GPT-OSS · final expanded cohorts

Context dependence remains the key limitation

At code onset, contextual summaries detect 459/462 violations with 0/259 false alarms; shuffled summaries detect 457/463 and blind summaries 0/464. On the template stress panel, summaries detect 16/18 versus original J-view 18/18. Denominators vary with missingness.

Scientific limits stay visible. The Oracle component is a verbalizer, not ground truth. Its targeted audit found conflicting and unsupported claims. Full reconstruction / FVE, broad transfer fidelity and real-model causal interventions remain unmeasured. These are conditional follow-up studies, not quietly counted as completed milestones.
